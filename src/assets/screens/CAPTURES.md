# Captures

Cleaned masters only. The raw `shot1`–`shot9-library` files are not in this repo. Each row stays `provisional: yes` until the Designer signs the slot and Gor approves the three. While a row is provisional the build may show the capture with `data-provisional="true"`. `check:launch` fails on any provisional row, any empty sign-off, or any `data-placeholder`.

Masters: `src/assets/screens/masters/`. Exports and the sha256 sidecars are written next to the images in `public/screens/` (and copied to `out/screens/` by the static export).
